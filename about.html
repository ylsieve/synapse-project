SYNAPSE

About the Project

Synapse was founded to bridge the gap between Behavioral Psychology and Computer Science.

As a middle school student, I noticed that most educational resources about social media are written by adults who don't understand our digital environment. I built this platform to give teens the tools to understand their own brains and contribute to real-world research.

The Mission

  • Education: Making cognitive science accessible to teenagers.
  • Research: Building a peer-led dataset on digital wellbeing.
  • Transparency: Using open-source code to ensure data ethics.
